02 · How an engagement runs合作流程

One workflow, shipped to real users, then handed over.先做一个流程，上线给真实用户，再交给你的团队。

Most AI projects stall because they start too wide. I start with the one task that costs you the most time, and I don’t leave until someone on your team owns it.很多 AI 项目卡住，是因为一开始铺得太大。我从最耗你时间的那一件事入手，直到你团队里有人能接手才离开。

Step 1

Sit in进现场

1 to 2 days1 到 2 天

I watch the work, collect real examples, and find where hours and customers are lost.看你们实际怎么干活，收集真实样本，找出时间和客户在哪里流失。

You get:交付： a one-page problem brief一页问题说明
Step 2

Map the risk画出风险

about 1 week约 1 周

Pick one workflow. Decide what the AI may read, write and send, and which number will prove it worked.选定一个流程。定清楚 AI 能读什么、写什么、发什么，以及用哪个数字证明有效。

You get:交付： permissions and a success metric权限表和成功指标
Step 3

Pilot小范围试点

2 to 4 weeks2 到 4 周

Ship a narrow version to real users with human approval switched on. Fix what they hit.把收窄后的版本交给真实用户，保留人工审批，用到哪里不顺就修哪里。

You get:交付： a working system and its logs能用的系统和完整日志
Step 4

Harden and hand over加固并交接

1 to 2 weeks1 到 2 周

Tests, audit logs, fallbacks for when the model is down, and training for the person who will own it.补齐测试、审计日志、模型宕机时的兜底方案，并培训以后负责它的人。

You get:交付： docs, runbook and an owner文档、运维手册和负责人
The AI drafts. A person approves.AI 起草，人来拍板。Anything that reaches a customer goes past someone on your team first.凡是发给客户的内容，都先经过你团队里的人。
Your data stays yours.数据始终是你的。Sensitive fields stay out of the model, and everything is exportable.敏感信息不进模型，所有数据随时可导出。
No lock-in.不绑定。Mainstream stack, swappable model providers, and code your next engineer can read.主流技术栈，模型供应商可替换，下一个工程师也看得懂代码。
